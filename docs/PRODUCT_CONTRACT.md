# Product Contract

Budget Shield is a native Android budgeting application presented as a game.

## Purpose

Answer this question:

> How much money can I safely spend right now while still paying every protected bill by its due date?

## Primary Result

**Safe Now**

Do Not Rename To:
- Cash available
- Balance
- Spending money
- Disposable income
- Cash on hand

## Core Product Features

### Onboarding
- Setup Quest onboarding

### Financial Tracking
- Income and payday scheduling
- Bills with exact due dates
- Recurring bill schedules
- Current cleared cash

### Core Calculations
- Safe Now calculation
- Next Payday information
- Bills Protected status

### Budget Categories
- Wants budget
- Food budget
- Savings goals
- Savings streak

### Activity
- Recent activity ledger
- Add income
- Pay bill
- Save money
- Bill-paid reward screen

### Progression
- Shield XP and progression

### Management
- Month switching
- Editable transactions
- Local persistent storage
- Export and backup planning
- Settings

### Deliverable
- Fully functional Android APK
